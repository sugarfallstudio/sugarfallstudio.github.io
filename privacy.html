Jelly Plunge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Jelly Plunge is a game for Android made by Sugar Fall Studio Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

The short version

Jelly Plunge does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no adverts, no analytics, no accounts and no online features. It works fully offline. It offers optional in-app purchases, which are handled entirely by Google Play (see below).

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, gems, unlocked items, purchases you have made, awards and best scores) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and accessibility options) in the game's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not connect to any server of ours. The only time it goes online is to talk to Google Play about in-app purchases.

Google Play

When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's own privacy policy. Google may provide us with anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports through the Google Play Console. These do not identify you.

In-app purchases

Jelly Plunge offers optional purchases such as gem packs and a skin pack. They are never needed to finish the game. Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the game which items you have bought, so they can be delivered and restored on a new device, and gives us order and sales reports for accounting and refunds. Purchases need the permission of the account holder; parents can require approval for purchases in Google Play's settings.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are made through the Google Play account on the device, where parents can require approval for every purchase.

Changes

If a future update adds adverts or any other feature that uses personal information, we will update this policy and the game's store listing before that update is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
