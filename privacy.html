Jelly Plunge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Jelly Plunge is a game for Android made by Sugar Fall Studio Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

The short version

Jelly Plunge does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no adverts, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no accounts and no online features. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, gems, unlocked items, awards and best scores) and your settings (sound, music, vibration and accessibility options) in the game's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not request any device permissions and does not connect to the internet.

Google Play

When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's own privacy policy. Google may provide us with anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports through the Google Play Console. These do not identify you.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update adds adverts, purchases or any feature that uses personal information, we will update this policy and the game's store listing before that update is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
